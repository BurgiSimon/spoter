# Trademark policy

The source code of Spoter is licensed under the GNU AGPL-3.0. The AGPL grants rights to the code,
**not** to the project's name, logo or visual identity.

- You may say that your project is "based on Spoter" or "a fork of Spoter".
- You may run unmodified Spoter for yourself or your community under the name Spoter.
- If you publicly offer a **modified** version, use your own name and logo, so users are not
  confused about who runs and supports it. Remember that the AGPL then requires you to offer your
  modified source code to your users (set `SOURCE_URL`).

Questions or permission requests: open an issue at https://github.com/BurgiSimon/spoter.
